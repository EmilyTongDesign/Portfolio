A Little More About Me

The Person Behind the Projects.

Hi! I'm Emily Tong, an Integrated Design & Media student at New York University Tandon School of Engineering, with minors in Mechanical Engineering and Mathematics.

My background in engineering shapes the way I approach problem-solving, while my love for art encourages me to experiment, explore, and create.

I'm particularly interested in industrial design, physical fabrication, and transforming ideas into thoughtful, tangible experiences.

Outside of design, I enjoy drawing, calligraphy, baking, playing video games, and making handmade gifts for people I care about.
